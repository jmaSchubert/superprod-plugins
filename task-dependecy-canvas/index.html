Task Dependency Canvas Help

  • Pan Canvas Click & drag on canvas background, or middle-click drag, or Hold Space + drag.
  • Zoom Scroll mouse wheel, or use + and - buttons on toolbar.
  • Place Tasks Drag tasks from the left sidebar and drop them anywhere on the canvas. Or click + on a sidebar card.
  • Link Tasks Drag from the blue right port (Blocks) of a prerequisite task to the yellow left port (Depends on) of a dependent task.
  • Highlight Chain Click any task to highlight all its upstream blockers and downstream dependents. Click background to deselect.
  • Delete Link Hover over any dependency arrow line and click the red ✕ button that appears.
  • Auto Layout Click ⚡ Auto Layout to arrange all tasks into clean dependency columns automatically.
